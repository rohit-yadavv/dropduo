Pair once. Stay paired.

Three steps, done once. After that, both devices remember each other and reconnect on their own.

  1. Open DropDuo on your Mac

    Choose Pair a device. A short-lived QR code appears.

  2. Scan it with your phone

    Both devices need to be on the same network. No codes to type, no account to create.

  3. Approve it on your Mac

    Nothing is trusted until you say so. You can unpair a device whenever you like.

Nothing in between.

  • Encrypted with a separate key each way
  • You approve every new device
  • No account, ads or analytics
