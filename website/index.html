Built for the things you actually send.

DropDuo lives in the Android share sheet. Photos, PDFs, a link from your browser: tap Share, pick DropDuo, it lands on your Mac.

Full-quality originals

Text, only on purpose

Picks up where it stopped

Trip to Kasol.mov

Pair once. Stay paired.

Three steps, done once. After that, both devices remember each other and reconnect on their own.

  1. Open DropDuo on your Mac

    Choose Pair a device. A short-lived QR code appears.

  2. Scan it with your phone

    Both devices need to be on the same network. No codes to type, no account to create.

  3. Approve it on your Mac

    Nothing is trusted until you say so. You can unpair a device whenever you like.

Your files go from one device to the other. That is the whole trip.

  • Encrypted on your network

    Every pair gets its own secret. Each session derives fresh keys before a single byte moves.

  • No account, no cloud

    No sign-up, no relay servers, no ads, no analytics. Nothing to log into.

  • Names are not identity

    A device is trusted because you approved it, not because of what it calls itself.

  • Open source

    Apache-2.0. The protocol is published for review and has not had an independent audit yet.
