# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique work, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors to protect the community. Reports should go through a private maintainer channel when available; do not publish personal information in an issue. Enforcement must be proportionate, documented privately, and allow an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when one exists.
